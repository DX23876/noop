# Getting help with NOOP

- **🐞 Bugs → open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/DX23876/noop/issues/new/choose).** There's a template, and bugs are tracked, deduped and linked to fixes here. Include your **NOOP version**, **platform** and **strap model**, plus a **strap log** — NOOP runs entirely on your device, so without one we usually can't tell a real fault from a setup detail. (iPhone, iPad and Mac: Settings → **Connection diagnostics** → *Strap log* → **Save…**/**Copy**; Android testing builds: Settings → **Help & Diagnostics** → **Share strap log**.)
- **💬 Questions, ideas & how-you-can-help → [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/DX23876/noop/discussions).** Ask, share results, and see [exactly what data would move NOOP forward](https://github.com/DX23876/noop/discussions/147).
- **📣 Setup help, tips & release news → [r/NOOPApp](https://www.reddit.com/r/NOOPApp/).** Every release is announced there too.
- **📖 Docs → the [README](README.md)** covers install (AltStore/SideStore on iPhone, macOS Gatekeeper), pairing a WHOOP 5.0/MG, privacy, and importing your history. The Android app is in testing; [docs/fork/android/TESTING.md](docs/fork/android/TESTING.md) explains how to install a testing APK and what to report.
- **📧 Other → thenoopapp@gmail.com**

NOOP is an independent, unofficial project — **not affiliated with WHOOP, Inc.** — and **not a medical device**.
