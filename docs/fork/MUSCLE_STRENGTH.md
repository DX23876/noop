# Muscle Strength

Muscle Strength answers: **Are the wearer's comparable exercise-performance trends for a muscle rising, stable or falling?** It does not assign a kilogram value to a muscle.

Eligible completed working sets produce RIR-corrected estimated one-repetition maxima where the exercise and repetition range support one. NOOP keeps one best point per exercise and session, then estimates a robust Theil–Sen trend. Every exercise is normalized against its own median e1RM before exercises are combined, so kilograms from a bench press and a row are never added.

Primary exercise-to-muscle evidence receives weight `1.0`; secondary evidence receives `0.5`. Warm-ups and stabilizers do not contribute. At least four usable points are required, and a trend whose central slope spread crosses zero is labelled unclear. Imports contribute when their exercise and set data can be resolved; unavailable RIR is not invented.

Colours describe the direction and confidence of the available exercise history. They do not measure isolated-muscle force, predict a competition maximum, diagnose weakness or prove hypertrophy.
