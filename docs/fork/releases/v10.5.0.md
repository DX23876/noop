# NOOP AI 10.5.0

This release folds in `ryanbr/noop` up to upstream v10.5.0 (129 commits since the 10.1.0 sync) and
carries the fork's own work — the AI coach, the persisted analyze-scan skip, the Today redesign —
forward unchanged.

## What upstream brings

- **Training load.** CTL (42-day chronic load), ATL (7-day acute load) and the form balance between
  them, as a Trends card with a build-up state until enough consecutive Effort days exist.
- **VO₂max without a waist measurement (#1391).** A resting-HR / age / sex estimate that names the
  method it used instead of presenting a single number as the only possible one.
- **Apple Health write-back (#1432) and hourly steps (#1429).** Workouts flow back into Health
  automatically through a best-effort background refresh task, and iPhone steps import hour by hour
  (new `appleStepHour` table) with a one-time 90-day backfill, so a phone that was dead for part of a
  day no longer just reads low for the whole day.
- **Oura read honestly.** The ring's own per-window breath rate is decoded as instrumentation and is
  refused by the sleep stager by provenance rather than by cadence luck (#1384, #1450, #1467), and
  Rhythm gives a truthful empty state on hardware that cannot support the reading (#1360).
- **Power saving moved out of Settings into its own screen (#1431)**, with a 35% trigger option and
  an hourly "Low refresh" mode. In this fork it is reachable from the More tab and the macOS sidebar.
- **Polar PMD support** (`Packages/PolarProtocol`), a widened `u32` fix set in the WHOOP decoder
  (#869), and the strap-model reporting fix that says "unknown" rather than guessing.

## What the fork kept

- The persisted analyze-scan skip (`dayScanFingerprint` / `analysisInputRevision`, migrations v38–v40)
  is kept instead of upstream's in-memory per-day cache (#1005): it answers the same question but
  survives relaunch and is invalidated by the write-side revision. Upstream's `appleStepHour`
  migration is registered as `v41-apple-step-hour` for that reason.
- Unbounded stream reads (`limit: Int.max`) on the sleep/Charge/Effort paths, the off-main-thread
  storage reads, the day-cycle backdrop defaulting to off, and the coach's semantic-memory background
  task and thermal/memory unloading.
