# NOOP Forge 11.9.0

DRAFT, written on `feature/goals` ahead of the release. The release prep adds the other changes since
11.8.5, runs `Tools/appchangelog-gen.py` on this file and translates the card.

## Goals for the week and the month

- **Weekly and monthly goals** beside the daily goals and the long-term goals: workouts, training
  minutes, distance, step days, nights of enough sleep, average sleep, zone 2+ minutes, working sets,
  active energy, rest days, hydration days and journal habits. A pace mark shows where you should be by
  today; the state reads Reached, Ahead, On track, Close, Behind, Out of reach, Protected or Starting.
- Rest days are set once in the training settings and every weekly goal plans around them. The week
  starts on the day chosen there.
- Results are frozen when a period ends, including the target that applied then. Streaks hold through an
  "Almost" period (80 %) and through paused periods.
- **My goals** is its own page: daily rings for up to three daily goals, then the weekly, monthly and
  long-term lists, badges, streaks and personal records, last week's review and ended goals. Reach it
  from the Goals section on every Today style, the top of More, the app icon's quick actions, the
  `noop://goals` link and, on the Mac, the sidebar entry under Today or Command-3. Command-N on the page
  starts a new goal.
- A first-visit explainer and suggestions sized from your own recent weeks; conflicting goals (two step
  goals with different numbers) are pointed out.
- **Goals where they count.** Training and Sleep show their goals at the top, a finished strength session
  shows what it adds before you save it, a workout's detail says which goal it counted toward and the
  night detail says which night of the week's sleep goal it was.
- The goal widget, lock-screen accessories, the watch complication and two Siri shortcuts ("How am I
  doing on my goals?", "Open goals").
- Daily goals tick themselves wherever NOOP can measure them, now including a journal entry ("Meditation"
  logged yes, "Alcohol" logged no). A box NOOP could not see done (ticked by hand, a journal entry not
  logged, a workout that was not recorded) is asked about the next day, "Done yesterday?", for up to three
  days. For a journal-linked goal the answer is logged in the journal; for a workout goal the workout can
  be added instead.
- Optional notifications for a reached daily goal, a streak at risk in the evening and a new badge: off
  until you agree once, at most two a day, never in quiet hours or after 21:00.
- **Coach.** The goal setup draft can now include up to three weekly or monthly goals, sized from your
  recent weeks. Nothing is created until you review and confirm it, on the goals page or in the Coach.
  Its routines are measurable wherever NOOP can measure them (a walk is a steps or Walking routine, a
  habit you log is a journal routine); a box to tick only for what nothing measures.
- Goals are part of the `.noopbak` backup.

## After the update

No reanalysis runs: goals read the scores and workouts already stored and change no scoring formula,
window or stored derived value. Analysis migration required: no.
