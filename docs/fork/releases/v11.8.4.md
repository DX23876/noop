# NOOP Forge 11.8.4

The first release under the name **NOOP Forge** (the home screen says "Forge"). It carries 70 changes
made in this fork since 11.8.3 and 217 commits from RyanBR upstream in three syncs. NOOP Forge is an
independent fork of NOOP by ryanbr, not the official app. Bundle identifiers, the App Group and the
store path are unchanged, so an installed app keeps its data, Health permissions and AltStore update
path.

## Name and look

- The app is called NOOP Forge, with new icons on iOS (light, dark, tinted), macOS and watch, and the
  Forge mark on Liquid Today.
- Cards come in four kinds (navigation, data, hero, state) with one shared chrome rule. On light
  backgrounds the Liquid hero stays a light card, Today's cards are quieter and shadows softer.
- More and Settings are organised as pages. Pink is used only where it means heart rate; Body has a
  large title.
- Accent colours used as small text are darker on light cards, and Increase Contrast draws a visible
  edge in light mode.

## Energy and calories

- **Energy model v8 and v9.** A session on foot is priced by its pace on the walking speed curve, with
  the existing bounded heart-rate correction. Other workouts are scaled to an aerobic ceiling that fits
  the body: an entered VO₂max, an Apple Watch reading, or the Jurca formula from the strap's resting heart
  rate and a measured activity level. Above a BMI of 40 a formula estimate carries a hint that a
  measurement is more accurate.
- An Apple Watch VO₂max reading now counts for six months instead of 30 days, converted from the weight
  at the time of the reading to the current one.
- **No session figure comes from Keytel any more.** One session price serves the tile, the live save,
  the manual rescore and the detector's backfill. Stored Keytel figures are recognised and corrected:
  - AI-13 corrects manual rows whose figure reproduces an old formula; typed figures stay.
  - AI-14 also reaches older live GPS workouts saved under the canonical workout owner, and keeps the
    repair pending when a registry or heart-rate read fails.
  - AI-15 reads each day's resting heart rate from NOOP's own daily rows as well as imported ones, and
    prices the rows it already marked again.
  Apple Health receives a workout's active share instead of the gross figure, and the corrected span is
  rewritten once. Analysis migration required: yes. AI-13, AI-14 and AI-15 correct stored session energy;
  they do not rescore daily analysis rows.
- Every surface that shows active energy reads the energy model, with the strap first and Apple only on
  a day it did not cover.
- One basal formula applies to the whole history, Mifflin–St Jeor by default. Katch–McArdle is
  recommended only with a DEXA body-fat reading.
- A new energy model, or a change to body data or the formula, recomputes the stored energy history once
  in the background. Multi-day recomputes no longer fail on a bucket filed under two days.
- **Nutrition from Apple Health.** Calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat are read as one daily total
  from one Health writer, so a meal mirrored by two apps is not counted twice; the Energy screen asks for
  a preferred writer when several are present. Manual and CSV entries win per field, and "No intake this
  day" is an explicit zero. A failed Health query never wipes the previous window.

## Training

- **Training Load, rebuilt.** The cardio lane is priced with Banister TRIMP and kept per session in a
  ledger. Each lane shows its history over months and years, reads one load band everywhere, and feeds
  Readiness and the Coach. Training Load adds room, an outlook, a rating quota and an opt-in load alert.
  Analysis migration required: yes, recipe AI-9.
- A VO₂max estimate from runs and walks is available as an experimental instrument, separate from the
  headline value.
- A strap double-tap completes the next set, the strap buzzes before rest ends, and the session explains
  why a tap was not logged.
- Later sets follow a changed weight or rep count; an exercise added mid-session starts from its last
  performance.
- Training weight has its own kg/lb setting. Saved weights stay in kilograms; increments, plates and
  equipment keep separate metric and imperial setups.
- **Apple Health workouts get average and peak heart rate, Effort and steps.** Heart rate comes from the
  band where it covered the session, otherwise from the workout's own Apple Watch minutes. Older workouts
  are filled in the background, newest first. Workouts the WHOOP app copied into Health stay as they came.
  Analysis migration required: yes. AI-16 fills stored Apple Health workouts once; it does not rescore
  daily analysis rows.
- The Workouts list reloads when stored session energy changes, and the workout detail shows its route,
  steps and a recorded calorie figure before the heart-rate charts finish loading.
- Session RPE is asked only where it adds something heart rate cannot; CrossFit stays a heart-rate
  session. Oura cloud sessions are read as Oura, in the list and the energy model.
- The Profile card opens read-only, and an automatic maximum heart rate shows its number.

## Analysis

- The daily resting heart rate stays the lowest five-minute bin, now of the primary session. Analysis
  migration required: yes, recipe AI-11.
- A WHOOP's R-R intervals are read from one delivery path per five-minute segment. Analysis migration
  required: yes, recipe AI-12.
- WHOOP 5/MG motion buffers are banked in Raw Data Collector sessions.
- The Coach's zone-minutes tool counts workout time only. It had summed every hour of the week, cut to
  the first two hours by a sample cap.

## After the update

The recipes AI-9 to AI-17 run once: the standard bounded 21-day reanalysis, plus one-time corrections of
stored workout energy and Apple Health workouts. Raw data and your own sleep and workout corrections are
never removed.
