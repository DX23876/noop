# NOOP Forge 12.1.0

About 65 changes made in this fork since 12.0.1 and one sync from RyanBR upstream. NOOP Forge is an
independent fork of NOOP by ryanbr, not the official app. Bundle identifiers, the App Group and the
store path are unchanged, so an installed app keeps its data, Health permissions and AltStore update
path.

## Workouts and training

- **Elevation on GPS workouts.** A GPS workout on iPhone reads the barometer beside the route and counts
  gain and loss from it; without a barometer (every Mac) no elevation is shown, never GPS altitude. The
  live screen has an Elevation page with gain, loss, current height and the grade over the last 50 m.
  The workout detail draws an elevation profile over distance and a grade-adjusted pace after Minetti et
  al. 2002. The training VO₂max estimate counts a known climb, from the barometer, Apple Health or an
  imported file, instead of treating every walk and run as level.
- **Start in one tap.** The Training tab's start card starts today's plan, freestyle or a recent
  activity directly, with the route and zone-coach choice that activity was last started with. The
  choice is printed under its name and a visible button changes it before the start. The tab now reads
  in the order it is used: start, goals, week, routines, recent sessions, then history and analysis.
- The target zone and the voice announcement intervals are picked from a bottom sheet, each zone with
  its heart-rate range.
- With the strength logger open, the rest-end notification only plays its sound; Settings › Training is
  grouped so the rest tone and the Lock Screen timer are easy to find.
- **Max heart rate suggestion.** When the strap has seen a higher heart rate held for 30 seconds on two
  days of the last six months, Settings offers it under Max heart rate. Effort already scored stays as
  it is.

## Settings

- Settings is 17 pages in six groups: You, Strap & Devices, Training & Everyday, Display, Data and Help.
  Every control has one page and other screens link there.
- **Calculations** gathers every switch that recomputes scores, with the repair tools and a warning that
  past days can change. **Lab** holds the unfinished features for every strap, so a WHOOP 4.0 owner now
  sees them too.
- Phone and strap heart rate sharing has its own screen under Strap. German says "Strap" throughout.

## AgeLess and Daily rhythm (Lab, experimental beta, off by default)

Both are experimental and in beta: estimates to motivate, not medical ages. Turn them on in Settings › Lab;
after the update the bell introduces each once and links to its switch. While a switch is on, its screen
also has a row under More › Health & Body.

- **AgeLess.** An age built from six months of nine measures of sleep, activity and fitness, each tied to a large
  mortality study, on a half-circle gauge around your actual age with **Aging speed** beside it. It takes
  Body Age's place on Health while it is on. Turn it on in Settings › Lab.
- Its screen ranks the biggest levers, and each one can be set as a goal on the same number. Every
  measure shows a 30-day trend and a line saying what it stands on, flagged when the data is thin. An
  **About AgeLess** sheet links each study.
- VO₂max counts only what was measured: a lab value you enter (for a year) or Apple Watch's readings
  (for six months). The estimate from walks did not track measured VO₂max in a check against 981
  treadmill tests, so long walks only show a lower bound beside it.
- Body fat below the lean-mass target no longer takes years off. The study behind the measure finds the
  lowest risk near 25 % body fat, so less than the target shows no further benefit; above it still adds
  years.
- Recovery contributors on Health use the same segmented bars.
- **Daily rhythm (new, WHOOP 5.0 and MG).** A rhythm age from how your day moves, after the CosinorAge
  study (Shim, Fleisch and Barata 2024, 53,218 people). It sits on the same gauge as AgeLess and shows
  your last seven days as 24 hourly bars against the study's typical day, how much you move over the day
  and how clearly day and night differ, your busiest hours and the part of the day where movement is
  missing. An About sheet explains how it differs from AgeLess and links the study.
- The strap's motion figure was matched to the study's measure on one week of raw strap data from one
  person, so very active wearers may read too low. It is scored once a day on your device and needs three
  days worn day and night.
- The experimental step filter comparison is gone from Lab. Against a step reference from a week of raw
  strap data it counted 23 % fewer steps and agreed worse than the current count, which stays.

## Today and the rest of the app

- **Next strap alarm** under the date on Today, only while the alarm is on and will be armed. Alarm times
  follow the Clock setting everywhere.
- **Progress while scores are recalculated.** After an update that brings an analysis migration, a small
  card at the foot of Today shows how far it is and disappears once it is done.
- The journal card, the "Looks like a workout?" card and the cycle card on Today appear again; a load
  that never started kept them hidden.
- The measurements under an ECG reading are computed again.
- Trends drops the "Week in review" card, which showed the range mean as if it were the week.
- Strength, Cardio and Stress no longer redraw while a Coach reply streams.
- GPS points of past routes move out of the preferences file into their own files. On one iPhone they
  were 61 of the file's 62 MB, and every unrelated setting rewrote all of it.

## After the update

One migration runs on first launch (AI-21 to AI-27, all AgeLess). It deletes the stored AgeLess weeks so
the next analysis pass rebuilds them on the new VO₂max sources, the climbs above, what each measure
stands on and the body-fat change; banked zone minutes are kept. Daily rhythm needs no migration: it
starts scoring the day you turn it on. VO₂max goals re-read their start once. No daily score is
re-scored. Raw data and your own sleep and workout corrections are untouched.
