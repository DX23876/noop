# NOOP Forge 11.8.5

A Today release. It carries 65 changes made in this fork since 11.8.4 and 29 commits from RyanBR
upstream in one sync. NOOP Forge is an independent fork of NOOP by ryanbr, not the official app.
Bundle identifiers, the App Group and the store path are unchanged, so an installed app keeps its
data, Health permissions and AltStore update path.

## Liquid Today, redesigned

- **Organic score rings.** The three hero vessels become living rings for Charge, Effort and Rest:
  soft lobes, a band of filaments in a haze, twinkling particles and a slow breath of about four
  seconds. Effort glows deeper from Moderate up on the same tempo. The rings grow with the phone width,
  answer phone movement (a display-only "React to device movement" setting) and count up once per
  launch, then only when the value changes. Size, centre and the number never move. In Light mode the
  hero is a light card.
- Each ring is one VoiceOver element with value, unit, Charge band, carried stamp and source. With
  Differentiate Without Color on, the Charge band word is written under the ring.
- A not yet computed Effort shows a faint static ring with "–"; a calculated zero still reads 0.
- **One-line header.** "Today" with a short date on the left, the NOOP Forge wordmark in the centre,
  plus and profile on the right. The wordmark opens Updates; while something is unread its F pulses in
  ember. A thin line under the wordmark runs while strap or ring history is read. The battery moved to
  the top of the plus menu, which also ends with "Customize home". At accessibility text sizes the
  title moves below the controls.
- **Momentum** is a slim card under the hero; a tap opens the full page and a left swipe hides the
  message for today. Once every message is hidden a small "View Momentum" link stays. Momentum now
  follows the swiped day instead of showing the previous day's Charge.
- **Key Metrics against your usual.** HRV, resting heart rate, breathing rate, Charge and Rest show a
  change against your 30-day normal, coloured only beyond one standard deviation. Effort, steps and
  calories show their 30-day average today and a change on finished days; weight shows its 30-day
  change. A fresh layout leaves out the scores the hero already shows; saved layouts are untouched.
- SpO2 on Liquid Today falls back to the last calibrated reading like classic Today, instead of "—".
- **Training Load on Today.** A new section under Key Metrics (also on classic Today) shows strength and
  cardio as one four-segment scale each, with the current band, your personal edges, the verdict and
  one recovery line. A tap opens Training Load. A normal breathing rate now counts as a night read in
  the recovery reading.
- **Day-cycle scenes.** New Alps and Coast scene sets, ten lights each across the day, chosen in
  Settings › Appearance › Day-cycle motif. Alps is the default; the painted meadow stays selectable.
  The day-cycle background is on by default on every screen, and the Updates inbox sits on it too.
- Calmer surfaces: opaque cards, pills and secondary buttons drop their resting rim (Increase Contrast
  keeps every edge). Sections sit 24 pt apart.
- Goals, Energy and the other sections use one outside section head. The goal ring shows progress,
  the route and week strips are labelled, counted goal units drop the decimal.
- Last Workouts lead with when ("Today", "Yesterday", the weekday), with the sport glyph in its family
  colour and Effort with its band word.
- Energy on Today is a compact summary that keeps its slot while today's figures load. Energy uses
  Apple's activity colours for its four parts.
- Stress shows one decimal of its 0 to 3 scale. Rows without a 0 to max scale (Fitness Age, VO₂max,
  Coach) no longer draw a partial arc.

## Elsewhere in the app

- The Sleep tab uses the organic Rest ring.
- Explore has search, a remembered All or With Data filter and collapsible categories, with a colour of
  its own for each metric.
- The year of consistency in Training fits the card on every iPhone.
- The activity status chip keeps its state colour and never wraps.

## Devices: one WHOOP entry

A new WHOOP MG near an old 5.0 could record nothing for a day: the app swapped the old strap in and
moved the new strap's history under it. The strap pin is now kept across launches, a strap is only
re-pointed after a confirmed handoff, and a serial id is never moved onto another serial.

Pairing a WHOOP asks whether it replaces the previous strap; if so it takes over the one WHOOP entry
that holds the import and computed history. Installs with extra WHOOP entries get a Merge or Keep
separate offer on Devices. A merge needs the typed confirmation word, writes a full backup to
Files › NOOP › Backups first and resumes after an interruption. Analysis migration required: no.

## Coach

Fixes from the 2 October Coach review:

- "Ask coach" on a card only reads what Data access allows; goals reach the Coach only with the planning
  grant.
- Large numbers in a tool call no longer crash the app.
- One recorded workout confirms at most one plan commitment, a paused goal stays paused when edited and
  plan reminders cover sessions that already exist.
- Leaving a conversation stops a reply in flight, custom providers use the configured auth header,
  voice recording stops cleanly and a refused send keeps the draft.
- The macOS conversation history opens at a usable size.

## From RyanBR upstream

**Sync through `6ce657305` (29 commits).** Illness alerts tied to the latest evaluated night, skin
temperature attributed by source, archived devices keep their day ownership at the lowest priority, the
Oura R-R channel chosen per hour, backgrounded re-scores spaced 30 minutes apart, the breathing check-in
using your resonance pace, weekdays beside vital dates, a still night sky in Light appearance, a
Today-only Key Metrics option and segmented control accessibility. Analysis migration required: no.

Not taken: the legacy WHOOP 5 R-R gap note, which explains nights the fork's R-R reading does not
withhold, and upstream's re-score admission queue, since the fork's merged analysis queue already
serialises passes.

## After the update

No reanalysis runs: this release changes no scoring formula, window or stored derived value. Raw data
and your own sleep and workout corrections are untouched.
