# NOOP AI 10.1.2

This release adds closed-loop haptic heart-rate guidance for workouts and Live Sessions, makes the
separate heart-rate ceiling react immediately, and improves the reliability of the on-device coach
memory for Chinese and Japanese text.

## Haptic heart-rate zone coach

- **Choose a target zone before starting.** Workouts and Live Sessions offer no coach or target zone
  1–5. The last selection is suggested next time, while existing users remain opted out by default.
- **Your profile remains the source of truth.** The coach uses the current `ProfileStore.hrZoneSet`,
  including automatically calculated zones and custom percentage or BPM boundaries entered in the
  profile.
- **The strap communicates direction without a screen.** After eight stable seconds, one short pulse
  confirms the target zone, two light pulses mean increase intensity, and three stronger pulses mean
  ease off. Outside-zone reminders are limited to once every 30 seconds.
- **Bad readings do not produce late instructions.** Missing or invalid heart-rate samples interrupt
  stabilization, and changing profile zones resets the coach against the new boundaries.
- **State survives an app restart.** The selected zone is stored in the existing backward-compatible
  active-workout snapshot and restored with the workout. Old snapshots continue to decode.

## Immediate heart-rate ceiling

- **The ceiling remains separate from zone training.** It can use a profile zone or an independent
  fixed BPM value and warns as soon as the smoothed live heart rate reaches or exceeds the limit.
- **Choose limited reminders or a continuous two-second warning.** Recovery is confirmed when the
  smoothed heart rate falls below the configured hysteresis boundary.
- **Urgent haptics have deterministic priority.** The ceiling overrides the zone coach, and the zone
  coach overrides adaptive Live Session haptics. Adaptive session analysis is still calculated and
  stored when its haptic prompts are suppressed.

The feature is intended as a training aid, not medical monitoring. It depends on a connected, worn
strap and current heart-rate readings, so it can react late or miss a reading.

## More reliable local coach memory

- **Chinese and Japanese text is tokenized into useful overlapping units.** A full clause no longer
  collapses into one keyword token, while Latin-script tokenization remains unchanged.
- **Semantic results keep their ordering.** Keyword fallback can add exact matches but can no longer
  demote the strongest semantic result or rescue a weak semantic hit into the top positions.
- **Long unsegmented notes are chunked by character.** Chinese text that has few spaces is indexed in
  bounded overlapping chunks instead of being truncated as one oversized word.

The embedding model remains the pinned on-device Nomic Embed Text v2 MoE Q4_K_M model. No personal
memory text is uploaded by these changes.
