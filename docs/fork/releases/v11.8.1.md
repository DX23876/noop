# NOOP AI 11.8.1

The last fork release was 10.1.2 on 17 August. This one folds in everything `ryanbr/noop` shipped
since then — up through their v11.8.0 — and carries forward a large body of the fork's own work:
a native training log, a redesigned Training Load / Cardio / Strength screen, and a reworked energy
model. Nothing here changes NOOP's offline contract: no server, no account, no telemetry.

## Native training & logging

The fork's biggest addition this release. A built-in logger for strength and cardio sessions,
backed by one shared exercise catalogue and a single muscle-group taxonomy used consistently by the
picker, the analytics and the load map (no more three slightly different lists). Highlights:

- **One body-based muscle picker** for choosing exercises, feeding the same taxonomy the load map
  and analytics read — credit given where it's due, with the MuscleMap licence notice moved to its
  own Acknowledgements screen and the obliques and tibialis finally represented in the catalogue.
- **Optional exercise animations**, downloaded on request rather than bundled, so the app stays
  light for anyone who doesn't want them.
- **One active session for every entry point.** Starting a workout from Live, Workouts, or a Quick
  Action resumes the same session; the running workout now shows on the Lock Screen and in the
  Dynamic Island, and live cardio recording continues while NOOP is backgrounded.
- **The strength logger reworked** around one session header, with a consistent sheet for a
  routine's "Add" and "…" actions.
- **Today's fix (native strength sessions):** a native strength session could be seen but not
  deleted — `deleteWorkout` matched on a natural key a native row never carried. It's deletable now,
  cascading its exercises, sets and any legacy "Strength Training" twin.

## Training Load, Cardio and Strength — redesigned

All three screens now share one design kit, one set of colour lanes for reading load at a glance,
and evidence-aware training load math, on top of the CTL/ATL/TSB foundation upstream introduced in
10.5.0 (`#1423`, `#1425`). Also: an improved Steps chart with an optional 30-day average card, and a
sleep-aware notion of "day" that follows your own sleep/wake cycle rather than the calendar clock
(`#1572`).

## Body weight and energy, reworked

- **Weight history and trends** get their own card.
- **A new daily energy model**: NEAT, a personal forecast curve, and active-only calibration replace
  the earlier estimate, with a WHOOP-first bucket model, an opt-in Apple Watch calibration step, and
  a retrospective adaptive estimate for days NOOP had to guess. Every number is source-aware — it
  says whether it came from WHOOP, Apple Health, or an estimate — and Coach can now discuss weight
  and energy directly.

## The coach

Upstream `ryanbr/noop` has grown its own bring-your-own-key coach in parallel with this fork's — the
two share the same base (`Strand/AI/AICoach.swift`, four-then-more providers) and this release
absorbs upstream's latest additions to it: streaming replies, persisted history, on-device voice
input, a scheduled morning brief, home-screen widgets and a Siri intent, a live model catalogue, a
dedicated coach settings screen, and a master switch to turn the whole thing off. Thanks to
**@Fanboynz** and **@evoveotech** for that work.

What stays fork-exclusive on top of that shared base: on-device semantic memory (hybrid keyword +
embedding retrieval, hardened this cycle — see `Tools/MemoryBench`, the dropped guaranteed
keyword-rescue slots, and Polish stopword support), the 26-tool consent-gated tool-calling framework,
Goals & Journey tracking, proactive coaching, and the optional Hevy routine/workout sync.

## Sleep and recovery, more honest

- **"May be incomplete" now tiers by how short the night actually reads**, not by thin motion
  coverage alone — a night that scored 10+ hours no longer carries the same warning as one that
  scored four.
- **The Sleep hero respects your Liquid Design setting** instead of always drawing the liquid gauge.
- Upstream's own sleep work lands too: bridged-night time corrections, and a stager that can read a
  night from heart rate alone when a WHOOP 5/MG bank no motion (display-only, never feeding
  baselines).

## What else `ryanbr/noop` brings (10.5.0 → 11.8.0)

- **A strap that syncs itself.** A Sync Strap Shortcut, a "keep screen on while syncing" option and
  a sync Live Activity for the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
- **Protocol depth.** WHOOP 5/MG 100 Hz IMU capture and labeled recording sessions, a stable
  `whoop-<serial>` identity for 5.0/MG straps, and further Oura decoding (skin temperature as a Key
  Metrics tile, `SetFeatureMode` diagnostics, duplicate-session keying).
- **Workouts, cleaned up.** The list splits into Current and Archived and discards sub-minute
  sessions (`#2286`, `#2287`); the auto-detection pipeline is unified behind one offline path
  (`#2188`, thanks **@tigercraft4**).
- **Charts read better and cost less.** Points space by the date they actually span instead of by
  index, tooltips were added where they were missing, and the live heart-rate tick no longer
  re-renders the Workouts or Sleep screens on every beat.
- **Breathing protocols**, a truthful "no data" state on hardware that can't produce a reading
  (`#1360`), and Polar PMD command-layer groundwork for future strap support.
