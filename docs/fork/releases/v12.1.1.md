# NOOP Forge 12.1.1

A fix release on top of 12.1.0 with four changes made in this fork. NOOP Forge is an independent fork
of NOOP by ryanbr, not the official app. Bundle identifiers, the App Group and the store path are
unchanged, so an installed app keeps its data, Health permissions and AltStore update path.

## Settings on iOS 17 and 18

- **Settings no longer closes the app.** Since 12.0.1, opening Settings on iOS 17 or 18 closed the app
  at once; iOS 26 was not affected. The new Settings landing page listed its rows through a Swift
  feature that only exists from iOS 26. The compiler accepts it for an iOS 17 target with a warning,
  and the older system then fails when the page draws.
- A second place of the same kind, the lift directions on the Training Load screen, is fixed as well.
- The local pre-push checks now fail when the iOS build warns that something needs a newer iOS than
  17, and a source test rejects the pattern behind this crash.

## Strap connection

- **Live heart rate that went silent is renewed.** A WHOOP 5.0 or MG link could report its heart-rate
  stream as running for hours while no heart rate arrived, and only a real disconnect brought it back.
  The app now renews the subscription when it was confirmed before the strap's handshake finished, and
  again when it stays silent for 90 seconds. The link is never dropped for this. This path is rare and
  has not yet been seen on a strap; the renewal lines in the log are the evidence.
- **Reconnecting after repeated drops works in the background.** When two connection failures in quick
  succession paused automatic reconnecting, the app was meant to leave a standing reconnect waiting.
  That never happened on Apple, so a backgrounded app stayed disconnected until it was next opened
  (49 minutes in one field log, during a recorded workout). The standing reconnect is now left in place.

## Workouts

- **Effort for workouts recorded without live heart rate.** A workout recorded while the strap link was
  down was saved without average, peak or Effort, although the strap hands over its own heart rate at
  the next sync. Average and peak already followed that trace; the daily analysis now also stores the
  Effort for such workouts. Workouts that already had heart rate are not touched.

Analysis migration required: no. The release itself changes no recipe; the Effort fill only adds values
to workouts that had none.
