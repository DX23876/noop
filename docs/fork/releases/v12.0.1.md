# NOOP Forge 12.0.1

A large release. It carries about 120 changes made in this fork since 11.8.5 and three syncs from RyanBR
upstream, through 12.0.0 and the 12.1.0 test beta. NOOP Forge is an independent fork of NOOP by ryanbr,
not the official app. Bundle identifiers, the App Group and the store path are unchanged, so an
installed app keeps its data, Health permissions and AltStore update path.

## ECG on the WHOOP MG

- **Full readings.** The strap records about 30 seconds of single-lead ECG. A countdown shows the seconds
  left, the live trace follows the finger contact and a running reading cannot be swiped away.
- **Readable traces.** Readings are drawn at standard paper scale (25 mm/s, 10 mm/mV). The analysis
  undoes the strap's own 1.5 Hz high-pass before measuring, rejects noise bursts as beats and averages
  the clean beats into one median beat.
- **Measurements.** Heart rate, rhythm regularity, RMSSD and the PR, QRS and QT intervals with QTc from
  the average beat. P onset uses a wavelet delineator and the T end the trapezium-area method; the
  measurement code was checked against the cardiologist annotations of the PhysioNet QT Database.
- **Apple Watch comparison.** Apple Watch ECGs from Health load on their own after the first time and
  reload by button or pull-to-refresh. A strap reading and a Watch reading can be compared side by
  side, and a plain-text copy of each compared pair stays on the device.
- **Saved readings** have their own entry under More and in the macOS sidebar and open offline.
- A notice before the first reading says plainly that this is not a medical device, that it has no
  regulatory approval and how the strap's lead differs from a clinical one. Nothing here is a
  diagnosis.

## Goals

- **Weekly and monthly goals** beside the daily goals and the long-term goals: workouts, training
  minutes, distance, step days, nights of enough sleep, average sleep, zone 2+ minutes, working sets,
  active energy, rest days, hydration days and journal habits. A pace mark shows where you should be by
  today; the state reads Reached, Ahead, On track, Close, Behind, Out of reach, Protected or Starting.
- Rest days are set once in the training settings and every weekly goal plans around them. The week
  starts on the day chosen there.
- Results are frozen when a period ends, including the target that applied then. Streaks hold through an
  "Almost" period (80 %) and through paused periods.
- **My goals** is its own page: daily rings for up to three daily goals, then the weekly, monthly and
  long-term lists, badges, streaks and personal records, last week's review and ended goals. Reach it
  from the Goals section on every Today style, the top of More, the app icon's quick actions, the
  `noop://goals` link and, on the Mac, the sidebar entry under Today or Command-3. Command-N on the page
  starts a new goal.
- A first-visit explainer and suggestions sized from your own recent weeks; conflicting goals (two step
  goals with different numbers) are pointed out.
- **Goals where they count.** Training and Sleep show their goals at the top, a finished strength session
  shows what it adds before you save it, a workout's detail says which goal it counted toward and the
  night detail says which night of the week's sleep goal it was.
- The goal widget, lock-screen accessories, the watch complication and two Siri shortcuts ("How am I
  doing on my goals?", "Open goals").
- Daily goals tick themselves wherever NOOP can measure them, now including a journal entry ("Meditation"
  logged yes, "Alcohol" logged no). A box NOOP could not see done (ticked by hand, a journal entry not
  logged, a workout that was not recorded) is asked about the next day, "Done yesterday?", for up to three
  days. For a journal-linked goal the answer is logged in the journal; for a workout goal the workout can
  be added instead.
- Optional notifications for a reached daily goal, a streak at risk in the evening and a new badge: off
  until you agree once, at most two a day, never in quiet hours or after 21:00.
- **Coach.** The goal setup draft can now include up to three weekly or monthly goals, sized from your
  recent weeks. Nothing is created until you review and confirm it, on the goals page or in the Coach.
  Its routines are measurable wherever NOOP can measure them (a walk is a steps or Walking routine, a
  habit you log is a journal routine); a box to tick only for what nothing measures.
- Goals are part of the `.noopbak` backup.
- **Each goal in its own shape.** Pace track, day dots, target columns, history bars and a status ring
  instead of activity rings, in the goal's own colour. The goal widget draws them the same way.
- **Long-term catalog.** Five long-term shapes with a walkthrough page per goal, templates, measures,
  start packs and several goals of one kind. Measured goals count the actual weigh-in; only the
  verdict uses the trend.

## Workouts

- **One start screen.** Live, Workouts, the quick action, the Home Screen action and the Training tab
  all open the same Start workout screen, with today's plan, free training and routines on top and
  activities below.
- **Apple-style live workout** with honest GPS: a GPS silence is bridged or skipped instead of drawn as
  a straight line, and a bridged silence no longer marks the split as a gap. Spoken splits, intervals
  and a recap. Pause, resume and end from the Lock Screen; pausing a strength session pauses its rest
  timer.
- **Set a session's span on the heart rate curve** afterwards, also for a workout logged after the
  fact. A saved edit shows before the history re-score finishes.
- Steps and calories in a workout show progress while loading.

## Widgets, watch and the rest of the app

- **Ten fitness widgets** for sleep, HRV, resting heart rate, Charge, Effort, steps, weight, the
  training week, consistency and vitals.
- **Watch app** reworked for the watchOS 10 page deck.
- **Settings** is a grouped hub with focused subpages.
- **Sleep** lists deleted nights with recompute and hide, and draws one canvas under the hero.
- Clearer Training Load, Strength and Cardio pages. The day-cycle sky reaches around the notch and
  Reduce Motion is respected.
- **Alarms:** one wake schedule for the strap alarm and the wind-down reminder; weekday times edit
  correctly.
- **iOS:** a silent reminder when NOOP has not synced the strap for three hours, and typed sleep marks
  through Shortcuts.
- **Import:** Italian WHOOP exports; Hevy CSVs that return to an exercise keep unique set numbers; a
  renamed workout in a newer lifting export replaces its old copy.
- **Experimental step filter: Settings → Profile → Compare new step filter** (WHOOP 5.0 / MG, off by default) opens a 14-day
  table of the current step count, a stricter filtered count and the phone's steps, per calendar day.
  The filter counts stretches of 60 steps or more in full, keeps shorter bursts only when longer
  walking is within three minutes, and adds back the first steps of each walk that the strap releases
  in one go. Your step count, Energy and scores do not change; the screen only shows the comparison.
- Choosing W in the steps detail no longer freezes the app.

## Health and energy

- NOOP's own replaced samples no longer count as deletions in Health, sleep samples get a sync version
  before HealthKit validates them, and launch stays responsive when the notification or Health
  service is slow.
- Energy re-prices only the days an offload or re-score changed, and Health syncs hourly.

## From RyanBR upstream

**Syncs through `9f98f811a` (12.0.0), `8e94d559b` and `18de275ef` (12.1.0 test beta).** Charge
baselines fold only nights inside the 21-day window, and an imported WHOOP history only seeds them
until your own nights are trusted (#2525). Respiration and Effort baselines enter Charge only once
usable. Apple Health heart-rate write-back keeps writing after a strap is re-paired (#2644). Stress no
longer lets a cancelled load overwrite a newer one. The 5/MG battery pack charge shows on the Devices
card. Thanks to @meta1971, @Zebsi235, @vishk23 and @ayiskakov for the MG ECG protocol work in #891 that
the fork's reading builds on.

Not taken: upstream's iOS product rename (the fork keeps its own bundle identifier), Android and the
parity tooling, and upstream's re-score queue, since the fork's analysis queue already serialises
passes.

## After the update

Two re-scores run once on first launch, each over the standard 21-day window: AI-18 for the windowed
Charge baselines and AI-20 for the not-yet-usable respiration and Effort baselines. On real data the
stored Charge dropped by about 16 points on average where old imported nights had held the resting
heart rate baseline. AI-19 repairs retained Apple daily projections without re-scoring. Raw data and
your own sleep and workout corrections are untouched.
