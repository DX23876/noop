# NOOP Forge 12.3.0

An analysis release on top of 12.2.1. NOOP Forge is an independent fork of NOOP by ryanbr, not the
official app. Bundle identifiers, the App Group and the store path are unchanged, so an installed app
keeps its data, Health permissions and AltStore update path.

**One full recalculation after the update.** This release carries the analysis recipes AI-29 to AI-32.
Every day with raw strap data is scored again and every derived table is rebuilt from the stored inputs,
once, whichever of those versions an install was on. Testers who ran the testing build of 26 October
(AI-31) run it once more for AI-32. Raw data and anything you entered or imported stay as they are.

## Effort, recalibrated (AI-32)

- **One fitted curve.** Effort no longer adds up Edwards' five heart-rate zones on a log scale. Every
  minute weighs more the closer it sits to your maximum, and the day's load maps onto the 0 to 100 scale
  with a soft cap near the top. The constants are fitted on WHOOP's own Strain for 3,760 workouts in 15
  exports that people shared in [#2438](https://github.com/ryanbr/noop/issues/2438): a brisk
  30-minute walk lands around 32, an hour in zone 4 around 83 and a 3:30 marathon around 97.
- **Light activity counts inside workouts only.** Below half your heart-rate reserve a minute adds to
  Effort only during a recorded, imported or detected workout. Hours of standing and walking around no
  longer add up to a moderate day; a day counts from 50 % of your reserve as zone 1 did before.
- **Your measured maximum heart rate.** NOOP takes the level you hold for five consecutive seconds in
  workouts on two days of the last 90, starts from the Gellish formula (192 minus 0.007 times age
  squared) until it has them, ignores a peak more than 25 bpm above the formula as an artefact and lets
  the level fall slowly after a quiet stretch. A value you enter in Settings always wins, and Settings
  shows where the value comes from. Live Effort, heart-rate zones, AgeLess, energy and the coach read
  the same number. The 30-second suggestion is gone.
- **Banister leaves Effort.** The Banister setting for Effort is removed. The Training Load cardio lane
  keeps its Banister load.
- **Lab: Strength in Effort.** Off by default. A rated strength session adds session RPE times minutes
  times 0.11 to the day it starts on, the median that WHOOP gave 419 strength sessions of nine
  contributors beyond heart rate. No rating, no share.

Thanks to @bartmuskala for the maximum heart rate resolver this builds on, to @pipiche38 for the review
and the sustained-peak measurement, and to everyone who shared an export for the fit: @andresbernalc,
@MatteoFari, @iacobazzi, @bartmuskala, @Sneheth, @JohnDeen99, @Deezinor, @pilleuspulcher-blip,
@katharinawuensche, @andctri, @anilet, @Endanger6922, @dofimn and @F7EZ.

Analysis migration required: yes (AI-32). Every day is scored again with the new curve; before that the
rebuild banks the workout peaks over the whole raw history.

## AgeLess and body composition (AI-29, AI-30)

- **Steps for AgeLess.** A WHOOP 4.0 sends no step count, so AgeLess never gave such a wearer an age.
  Steps now come from the app's step resolver (strap count, then Apple Health, then the motion estimate).
  A week without an age names what is missing instead of always saying "after 30 nights".
- **Body fat and lean mass from Apple Health.** A scale's body fat and lean mass written to Apple Health
  reach AgeLess, the Katch-McArdle basal rate, the Energy plan, the Body page, body goals and the coach.

Analysis migration required: yes (AI-29, AI-30). The AgeLess weeks are rebuilt; on Katch-McArdle the
120-day energy window is priced again.

## Sleep, energy and widgets

- **Delete a wrongly detected nap.** Each nap has a menu with Delete nap, confirmed first, with the same
  few seconds of undo as a night. The main night of that day keeps its numbers.
- **Energy verdict.** Five kilocalories no longer flip today's verdict between "Still open" and
  "Heading for a surplus"; the day is too early only while the forecast reaches past the maintenance
  band on both sides.
- **Widgets without an App Group.** Apps signed with a wildcard profile (Feather and other certificate
  signers) cannot share data with their widgets. The Home Screen widgets now say so instead of showing
  empty rings.

Analysis migration required: no.

## ECG on the WHOOP MG (from upstream)

- **No stop commands during a reading.** A safety net that stops an orphaned motion stream also caught
  the ECG stream, which arrives under the same packet type, and sent the strap a stop every 30 seconds
  for as long as a reading ran. A well-formed ECG record is now exempt. The recording carried on before
  too, so no reading was lost. Measured by @ayiskakov.
- **The banked ECG record is kept.** The strap also banks each reading to its flash and sends it with the
  history. NOOP decodes that record and keeps it in its own table (migration v81, at most 36,000 records
  per strap, about 58 MB) instead of letting the 5 MB raw archive evict it within hours. Nothing reads it
  yet. Thanks to @meta1971 for the data behind the sample width and the cap.
- The record's alignment word is no longer decoded as a 101st sample.

Analysis migration required: no.

## Clock changes in the strap log

A day is scored as 24 hours from local midnight, so on a clock change one hour lands in the
neighbouring day. That stays as it is; the strap log now records it on the two days a year it happens
(`day-window-skew`). No value moves.

Analysis migration required: no.

## Upstream

Merged ryanbr/noop through 2a1de7609. The women's Banister multiplier (0.86 instead of 0.64) does not
apply to the fork's Effort any more; see the Training Load note below.

## One recalculation for every analysis change

- **The whole history is scored by the current version.** After this update NOOP calculates every day
  again that still has raw data from the strap, then the weekly scores (Fitness Age, Vitality, Body Age),
  its own stress values, the daily rhythm, workout heart rate, cardio load, energy and the AgeLess weeks.
  Before, each analysis change recalculated only the days it could prove affected, often the last 21, so
  older days kept the numbers of whichever version scored them last. From now on every analysis change
  runs this same full recalculation, at most once per release.
- **It takes longer once.** With about three months of strap data the first recalculation runs for 8 to 15
  minutes after the update on an iPhone 13 Pro Max and uses about 5 % of the battery. Keep NOOP open
  while it runs; if it is closed before it finishes, it continues where it stopped.
- **What stays.** Raw strap data is never changed, and nothing you entered or imported is replaced. A
  night you corrected keeps your bed and wake times; only its sleep stages are worked out again. A workout
  from Apple Health that came without heart rate gets it from the strap, as recent workouts already did.
  Days with an imported WHOOP stress value keep it.
- **Apple Health.** With sleep or vitals export turned on, NOOP sends the recalculated values for the
  whole period once.

Analysis migration required: yes (AI-31). The rebuild mechanism re-scores every day an older version or
a narrower window left behind. The sleep-onset change below is included in the later AI-32 rebuild.

## SleepStager V3: a more conservative start to a detected night

- Short quiet bouts separated by movement no longer pull the start of an independently qualifying
  later sleep run hours earlier. The detector uses the existing session-duration floor rather than a
  fixed bedtime. This can leave out a genuine short initial sleep.
- Short interruptions inside an established night still join the night, and a night made entirely of
  short fragments can still be recovered. Light, deep and REM formulas are unchanged.
- The full recalculation applies the new window selection to stored raw data. Imported and manually
  corrected bed/wake times remain authoritative; raw samples are unchanged.
- SleepStager V3 is the default pipeline, using this conservative onset policy with the unchanged V2
  phase engine. Settings retains V2 with the original detector and V1 with classic phases as explicit
  fallbacks. Switching methods recalculates recent nights; corrected bounds remain locked. An explicit
  legacy V1 choice is preserved, while the former default V2 choice upgrades to V3. The pipeline choice
  is included in detection caches, persisted daily signatures and backup settings. Android exposes V3
  when shared-core reads are enabled; its legacy Kotlin screens retain their V1/V2 choice.

Analysis migration required: yes, included in the current AI-32 full rebuild.

## Training measurements and exercise discovery

- Native workouts and reviewed Hevy routines support weight with duration and weight with distance.
  Assisted and added bodyweight prescriptions retain their load meaning. Timed and distance sets no
  longer acquire repetition targets from a newly created routine or a repetition progression policy.
- The Android exercise library includes a graphical muscle picker and saved available equipment.
  Every required tool must be available; bodyweight remains available without equipment.

Analysis migration required: yes, included in the AI-32 rebuild for corrected measurement
interpretation. Existing exercise definitions, completed logs and explicit imported prescriptions are
preserved. The library controls and credential revocation changes require no analysis migration.

## Whole-session ratings

- Android can ask for a whole-session rating about 30 minutes after training, with the same Off,
  When useful and Always choices as Apple. The local worker checks again before displaying a prompt,
  so a rating, deleted session or changed choice can suppress an earlier scheduled request.
- A native workout's deliberate rating and completed sets are saved together. A failed rating write
  keeps the draft available rather than leaving an incomplete completion.
- The Apple completion screen shows the editable session rating once and uses the canonical duration
  used by training history; the original completion snapshot no longer competes with a later edit.

Analysis migration required: no. This changes notification scheduling, transaction safety for explicit
input and its presentation; no historical rating or derived load is rewritten.

## Optional offline exercise media on Android

- Exercise details and the native workout logger can display local demonstrations, including still
  presentation under quiet motion. Large, Small and Hidden choices are available in the media page.
- The source, estimated size, rights holder and conditions are shown before a separate download can
  start. No images or animations are bundled. Downloads use the operating system's download service;
  the shared core validates and installs the archive in backup-excluded private storage.
- Media can be disabled or deleted independently of exercises, plans and completed workouts. A bad
  archive cannot replace a working pack; paths, actual bytes, CRCs and SHA-256 provenance are checked.

Analysis migration required: no. This adds optional presentation and local media management without
changing stored exercise identities, workout inputs or analysis. Swift Crypto 4.5.2 is pinned for shared
checksums; resolving the core now requires Swift 6.1 or later.

## Personal exercise provider review

- Apple and Android share one explicit HTTPS metadata request and exercise parser. Endpoint, optional
  key header and personal key remain configurable; loading a page does not save exercises or fetch media.
- Android previews the received definitions and saves only the selected items. Credentials use encrypted
  device storage. Replaced credentials on Apple retain the previous key if a Keychain update fails.
- Response buffering is bounded, redirects are rejected and requests carry no workout or health data.
  Definitions from the standard ExerciseDB endpoint preserve the required AscendAPI attribution.
- Bodyweight equipment aliases resolve correctly. A corrected measurement gets its own exercise identity
  when an older incompatible definition exists; completed logs keep their original interpretation.

Analysis migration required: no. Only explicit metadata imports gain corrected definitions; existing
measurement identities, historical workouts and derived loads remain unchanged.

## Shared goals foundation (local parity work, not yet a completed Android feature)

The existing goal models, daily actions, weekly/monthly evaluation, catalog assignment, safety checks,
measured-input helpers and pure progress engine now compile from shared Swift sources. Apple keeps its
existing preference format. The core has a private transaction image backed by additive SQLite migration
v79; multi-store changes commit together, stale writers are rejected and failures retain the previous image.
The shared refresh also owns daily completion, goal progress, milestone decisions and completed-period
settlement. Android exports the existing Apple-readable `goals.bundle` from the same SQLite snapshot it
archives. Imports prepare those settings in a disposable source copy before swapping the live database;
preparation failures leave the existing store and picked backup intact. Android goal data loading,
operations and the goal screens remain in progress.

Analysis migration required: no. The extraction preserves goal math and stored user inputs; no historical
analysis or user-entered target is rewritten.

## Active-energy inputs for Android goals (local parity work)

Daily and weekly energy goals now read the shared energy summary over stored device energy, Health
imports, step/stride coverage and logged activity, with historical body mass and the selected formula.
Apple delegates its summary and supporting reads to the same core sources. Missing measurements remain
absent. Forecast-only inputs are not read for goal totals.

Analysis migration required: yes, included in the AI-32 rebuild. The Android analysis adapter
now receives the Energy Plan's formula selection, including its untouched Mifflin–St Jeor default,
instead of silently using the generic UserProfile default. The existing energy-history rebuild covers
the corrected derived values; no new recipe number is added and raw/user inputs are retained.

## Shared goal readers and Android Momentum (local parity work)

Journal folding, hydration history and Explore source selection now use shared readers. Long-term goals
read valid manual/Health VO2max and computed sleep consistency through the same assembly on both hosts.
Existing Android Room journal/water input migration remains open; habit and hydration setup stay gated
until those inputs and their settings reach the core. Analysis migration required: no for these extractions.

Android now presents the shared Momentum feed on Today and in More. Core-generated copy uses the Apple
catalog translations. Loading, read failure and an empty feed have distinct states; hiding a message uses
the shared logical-day rule. Presentation bookkeeping preserves timestamp precision, and returning to the
foreground or crossing a day boundary refreshes the feed. Only the implemented Goals destination is
shown. Full Today context, the other destinations and rendered acceptance remain open.
Analysis migration required: no; this adds read models and presentation, not historical scoring.

## Android journal and water ownership (local parity work)

The shared database adopts the complete legacy Room journal and hydration snapshot once, preserving source
ids, exact question keys, notes, false answers and numeric zero. Existing core values win collisions. The
snapshot and its ownership marker commit together; failure leaves adoption retryable. Old Room inputs are
retained for recovery, and new repository reads/writes use the core rather than mirroring to Room.
Restoring a backup seals this lane on the staged database, so this phone's old Room rows cannot overwrite or
recreate restored/deleted user inputs. Backup export waits for adoption before taking its snapshot.

GRDB migration v80 is additive. Analysis migration required: no; source input ownership and transport only.
Catalog/opt-in settings, device source lifecycle and the remaining classic Room data lanes remain open.

## Android habit and hydration goals (local parity work)

Weekly, monthly and long-term goals on Android can now count a journal habit (do it or avoid it) and
hydration days. The goal setup offers the journal's questions; habit goals carry the habit's name as on iPhone.
Settings has a Today & Trends page with the water and journal card switches; turning water on unlocks
hydration goals. Analysis migration required: no; settings transport and goal setup only.

## Android inputs owned by the shared core (local parity work)

Workouts, Health Connect and Apple Health days, lab markers, live sessions, imported series, imported days and
nights, sleep edits, naps and day ownership now also live in the shared core, taken over once from the phone's
existing data and kept in step on every change, so the core's analysis, goals and backups see them as on iPhone.
Deleting or forgetting a strap clears its data there too. Analysis migration required: no; raw inputs only.

## Android Energy, AgeLess and daily rhythm (local parity work)

Android gets the Energy page (the three-route maintenance band, today's intake against the day's forecast, the
weekly target, activity level and basal formula) and, while their Lab switches are on, the AgeLess and Daily
rhythm pages. Every number comes from the shared core, so it matches the iPhone. Analysis migration required: no.

## Android Body page (local parity work)

Android gets the Body page: weight and weigh-ins, body fat with the tape estimate on its own line, and every tape
measurement with its change and how to take it. The numbers come from the shared core. Analysis migration required: no.

## Android coach history and plan (local parity work)

The Android coach chat now answers through the same coach as on iPhone. Two buttons in its header open the saved
conversations (search, switch, rename, pin, archive, delete) and the coach's plan book (accept or pass on a
suggestion, mark a session done or say why it did not happen, rate how it felt). Analysis migration required: no.

## Android Energy detail, body photos and saved ECGs (local parity work)

From the Energy page, Android opens the energy detail: a chosen day's total with its basal, movement and training
share, the burn rate through the day against the day before or a 7- or 30-day median, and the last 30 days. The
Body page gains progress photos, picked from the gallery, copied without camera metadata and kept on the device
only. Saved WHOOP MG ECGs, for example from a restored backup, show with their strip and measurements under
Health & Body; recording an ECG on Android is not available yet. Analysis migration required: no.

## Android Lab: training-based VO₂max (local parity work)

The Lab switch for the training-based VO₂max now exists on Android with the iPhone's key; Training Load then shows
the experimental estimate with how it compares with Apple Watch. Analysis migration required: no.

## Android Today energy, plan times, sleep balance (local parity work)

Today on Android shows the Energy card. The coach's plan book asks for a time when you accept a session and lets you
change the time, move it to another day or swap the activity. The Sleep tab shows the sleep balance over the last 14
nights, as on iPhone. Momentum's suggestions open the plan and Live as well as goals. For testers, a Lab switch lets the
classic screens read each day's values from the shared core, with a comparison against the phone's own copy.
Analysis migration required: no.
